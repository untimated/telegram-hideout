How to hang out

  • Drag the scene with a mouse or finger to look around.
  • Use WASD, arrow keys, or the on-screen joystick to move.
  • Push the joystick further to walk faster.
  • Press Enter to focus the chat box; press Enter again to send.
  • Tap things (or aim and press E) to use them: the bar, buffet, refreshments, jukebox, served food, other players.
  • Drinks make you drunk, food refuels you. Pass out and you are stuck until the hour, or until a friend splashes you awake.
